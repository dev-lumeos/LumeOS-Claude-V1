   - WHOOP (0-100%), Oura Readiness, Garmin Body Battery

3. **HRV = Gold Standard for Recovery Measurement**
   - All major wearables use HRV as primary recovery indicator

4. **Wearable Lock-in Frustration**
   - WHOOP costs $30/month, Oura $6/month, Garmin requires $300+ watch
   - Users frustrated by hardware requirements for basic recovery tracking
   - Opportunity for software-first recovery solution

5. **No App Combines Recovery + Training Load + Nutrition + Sleep**

   - Oura knows sleep but doesn't know your protein intake or training split
   - Zero comprehensive recovery platforms exist

### Recovery Science Research

#### HRV (Heart Rate Variability) Science
- **Physiological Basis:** Measure of autonomic nervous system balance
- **Recovery Indication:** Higher HRV typically indicates better recovery
- **Measurement Methods:** ECG (gold standard), PPG (wearables), smartphone camera (emerging)
- **Individual Baseline:** Requires personal baseline establishment over time
- **Training Integration:** HRV-guided training adjusts intensity based on readiness

#### Sleep Recovery Science
- **Sleep Stages:** Deep sleep most important for physical recovery
- **REM Sleep:** Critical for cognitive recovery and memory consolidation
- **Sleep Debt:** Accumulated deficit affects performance and recovery
- **Sleep Quality Metrics:** Duration, efficiency, wake frequency, sleep stages
- **Recovery Optimization:** Temperature, light exposure, timing protocols

#### Muscle Recovery Science
- **Protein Synthesis:** 24-48 hour window for optimal muscle repair
- **Muscle Group Specificity:** Different recovery times for different muscle groups
- **Training Load Impact:** Volume, intensity, and frequency affect recovery needs
- **Nutrition Impact:** Protein intake, carbohydrate timing, micronutrient status
- **Individual Variation:** Genetics, training history, age affect recovery rates

### Recovery Protocol Research

#### Evidence-Based Recovery Strategies
- **Sleep Optimization:** 7-9 hours, cool environment, consistent schedule
- **Nutrition Timing:** Post-workout protein within 2 hours, carbohydrate replenishment
- **Hydration Management:** Adequate fluid replacement after training
- **Active Recovery:** Light movement on rest days for blood flow
- **Stress Management:** Meditation, breathing exercises, stress reduction

#### Training Load Management
- **Periodization:** Planned recovery weeks and deload periods
- **Volume Progression:** Gradual increase in training volume over time
- **Intensity Cycling:** Alternating high and low intensity training days
- **Movement Quality:** Emphasis on proper form to reduce injury risk
- **Individual Adaptation:** Customization based on recovery capacity
