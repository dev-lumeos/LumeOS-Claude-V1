### Critical Market Gaps

1. **NO Platform Connects Coach Tools with Deep Health Data**
   - Trainerize tracks workouts but coaches can't see client's micronutrient gaps
   - Everfit has AI insights but no supplement stack analysis
   - TrueCoach integrates Oura but not comprehensive nutrition or medical data
   - **Massive opportunity for first platform with complete health picture**

2. **Coach Workflows Are Training-Centric**
   - All platforms focus on workout assignment and progress tracking
   - Nutrition coaching is basic (macro tracking at best)
   - Recovery coaching limited to sleep/HRV data visualization
   - No platform enables comprehensive health optimization coaching

3. **Client Data Is Fragmented**
   - Clients use separate apps for nutrition, supplements, medical tracking
   - Coaches get incomplete picture of client's health and progress
   - No single platform where coach can see everything affecting client performance

4. **AI Coaching Insights Are Basic**
   - Everfit has "AI insights" but they're simple pattern recognition
   - No AI analysis of cross-module relationships (training + nutrition + recovery)
   - Coaches still need to manually identify connections between different health factors

5. **Permission Systems Are Binary**
   - Most platforms: coach has access or doesn't
   - No granular permissions (coach can see nutrition but not medical data)
   - Client control over data sharing is limited

### B2B Coaching Workflow Research

#### Typical Coach-Client Interaction Patterns
1. **Initial Assessment:** Comprehensive client evaluation and goal setting
2. **Program Design:** Custom training and nutrition program creation
3. **Regular Check-ins:** Weekly/bi-weekly progress reviews and adjustments
4. **Progress Monitoring:** Continuous tracking of client metrics and outcomes
5. **Program Adjustment:** Regular modifications based on progress and feedback
6. **Communication:** Ongoing support, motivation, and education

#### Coach Types and Needs
- **Personal Trainers:** Focus on exercise programming and form coaching
- **Nutrition Coaches:** Specialized in dietary planning and macro coaching
- **Health Coaches:** Holistic approach including lifestyle and behavior change
- **Online Coaches:** Remote coaching requiring comprehensive digital tools
- **Hybrid Coaches:** Combination of in-person and online coaching services

### Cross-Module Integration Research

### Permission System Research

#### Current Permission Models
- **All or Nothing:** Coach has complete access or no access to client data
- **Platform Dependent:** Different permissions on different platforms (training app, nutrition app, etc.)
- **Limited Granularity:** Basic categories without detailed control

#### Lumeos Permission Strategy
1. **Granular Control:** Client controls exactly what health data coach can access
2. **Module-Specific:** Permissions set separately for nutrition, training, supplements, recovery, medical
3. **Time-Limited:** Permissions can be granted for specific periods
4. **Revocable:** Client can revoke permissions at any time
5. **Audit Trail:** Complete log of what data coach accessed and when
